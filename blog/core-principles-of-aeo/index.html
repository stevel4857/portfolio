Core Principles of AEO

SEO is important but now so is AEO — Answer Engine Optimization.

As AI assistants and answer engines become the primary way people discover information, the rules are shifting. It's no longer just about ranking in Google. It's about becoming the source that AI systems trust and cite.

What This Means for Your Website

  • Clear, authoritative, well-structured content wins
  • Technical accuracy and depth matter more than ever
  • Being the original source (not just aggregating) is powerful

The organizations that will thrive are those whose websites are built with both humans and intelligent systems in mind.
