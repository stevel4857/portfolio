About me

I'm a product designer with a decade of experience building digital tools that people genuinely enjoy using. I believe great design is invisible — it quietly removes friction and creates moments of delight.
My practice sits at the intersection of systems thinking, user research, and high-fidelity craft. I've led design for early-stage startups that scaled to millions of users, as well as enterprise products used by creative teams at the world's most ambitious companies.
