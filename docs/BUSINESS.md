## How clients pay (Steve was new to this — document clearly)

**Not a store.** No cart required on the website for project work.

1. Conversation / discovery  
2. Agree scope + price (proposal or short SOW)  
3. Client approves (email OK or e-sign)  
4. **Invoice** for deposit  
5. Payment clears → work starts  
6. Further invoices by milestone  
7. Care plans: monthly invoice or Stripe subscription
