I was asked to make a birthday card for Erin.

The brief was specific: Matrix style, she needed to look like herself, and her name and birthdate had to live on the cover.

Not a template. Not a rack card. Something that feels like the person who will open it.

Cover: Happy Birthday Erin — 7.21.2026
Inside: We love you. You kick reality’s ass.

Cory and I (and our cats, Nani and Pumpkin) even made it onto the back panel.

Oh — and I can easily make short videos to go with a card like this. A few seconds of motion makes the print feel like it jumped out of the movie.

Same craft as the web work I usually write about: listen to the brief, protect the likeness, land one clear emotional hit.
